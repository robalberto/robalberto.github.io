# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, potential collaborators, and peers in the AI and cybersecurity communities looking to understand Alberto's expertise and background.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio and online resume for an AI Researcher & Engineer. It serves to showcase technical skills (fine-tuning, deploying models), experience, education, and provide contact information. Success is defined by visitors easily grasping technical depth and knowing how to reach out.

## Brand Personality
Technical, Clean, Creative, Bold. 

## Anti-references
- SaaS Cliché (generic hero metrics, identical card grids, floating abstract 3D shapes without meaning).

## Design Principles
- **Show, don't tell:** Demonstrate technical depth through clear, focused content and precise layout, avoiding empty buzzwords.
- **Precision with personality:** Maintain a clean, highly readable foundation while allowing bold, creative accents to break up academic monotony.
- **Content is the interface:** Let the actual experience, projects, and writing drive the visual hierarchy rather than decorative wrapper elements.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Strict adherence to WCAG AA contrast standards, particularly ensuring text remains highly legible against the dark background.
- Support for keyboard navigation and screen readers.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for any creative animations.
